Before You Register
  • Provide your own valid email address and mobile number — these will be used for all official NIELIT communication.

  • Your account requires admin approval before you can log in and enroll in courses.
Password Guidelines
  • Minimum 8 characters required
  • Adding uppercase, numbers & special characters is recommended for a stronger password, but not mandatory
  • Avoid using your name, email or mobile number as password
